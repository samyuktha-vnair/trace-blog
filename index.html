Trace is a video interview series with the people who move food from field to fork — and study what it does once it gets there.

Each conversation follows one thread of the food system: agriculture, manufacturing, product development, marketing, food security, nutrition, medicine, and food science — and how those threads knot together.

About Trace

Hi, I'm Samyuktha — the person behind the camera on Trace. I've been a food security advocate since the fifth grade, when I first started working with the American Dairy Association, where I've since spoken on their panels as well as a panel for the Food Research & Action Center (FRAC).

More recently, I directed a food science documentary through Rutgers' FAME program, which is where the idea for Trace really started: the best explanations of how our food system works come from the people inside it, on camera, in their own words.

I'm currently a BAIT (Business Analytics & Information Technology) major with a Food Science minor at Rutgers, and I'm especially interested in agriculture technology, workflow development, and food science — which is a lot of why this project exists in the first place.
